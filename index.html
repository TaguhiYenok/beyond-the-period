About the Data

The existing project indicators are loaded from data/beyond_the_period_unicef_menstrual_health_all_years.csv. The file's upstream source and processing have not yet been verified; its filename alone does not establish UNICEF/WHO JMP provenance.

The separate World Bank menstrual material use indicator is loaded from data/world_bank_menstrual_material_rates.csv. See the World Bank indicator page for its published title and details. Although the measures are similar, their values are kept separate and may differ.

The period-pad price indicator uses the 2024 report, The Cost of Having a Period in 2024 . It compares the cheapest pad prices found in 30 countries, converted to U.S. dollars, and estimates lifetime cost using 11,400 pad uses. The report says prices were collected through online searches of local retailers; they may not represent current prices or everyone’s costs. The map shows pad price, while country details and map hovers also show estimated lifetime cost.

The year selector allows users to explore years with values for the selected indicator. Countries without available values for that year are not assigned values from another year.

Data availability varies across countries and indicators. The selected source and data year are identified on the map.

Missing data does not mean that access is zero.
