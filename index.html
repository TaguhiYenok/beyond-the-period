About the Data

This dashboard uses country-level menstrual health indicators from the UNICEF/WHO Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP).

The year selector allows users to explore available estimates for a particular calendar year. Countries without available estimates for that year are not assigned values from another year.

Important: JMP estimate years do not necessarily correspond to the years in which the underlying surveys were conducted. Data availability also varies across countries and indicators.

Missing data does not mean that access is zero.

Source: UNICEF/WHO JMP 2025
