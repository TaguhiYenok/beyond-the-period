Menstrual health is an important part of health, education, and everyday life. However, access to menstrual products, private spaces, and appropriate sanitation facilities varies around the world.

Explore country-level data to better understand menstrual health inequalities and the challenges people face in different regions.

About the Data

This dashboard uses menstrual health indicators published by the UNICEF/WHO Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP).

Data availability and reporting years vary between countries. Missing values indicate that an estimate is unavailable, not that the percentage is zero.

Source: UNICEF/WHO JMP 2025
